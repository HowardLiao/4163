07　Four plans for Intai

Connect the systems, deal with Notes, bring in AI, with security as the baseline

Plan 1

Connect PLM, SAP and MES into one data flow

The principle is to standardize the core and keep extensions outside, using SAP standard functions wherever possible to cut customization debt. Connect the data first, then talk about AI.

PLMDesign & BOM
SAPOrders, materials, cost
MESWork orders & reporting
QualityLot traceability
BI war roomManagement decisions

One less manual export

Find every process that relies on Excel and re-keying between systems, and replace each with an interface.

Consistent part numbers and BOMs

PLM design changes flow automatically to SAP and MES, cutting the risk of using the wrong revision.

Real-time stock and yield

Stock, yield and downtime at the Nantun and Houli plants on one set of metrics.

Plan 2

Don't replace Notes in one go; find each app a home in waves

1

Inventory

List every Notes database and workflow: purpose, users, frequency, owner and data volume.

2

Classify

Put each app in one of three groups: keep (for now), move (to SAP or a new platform), retire (archive, then shut down).

3

Migrate in waves

Start with high-use, high-risk apps; return functions that duplicate SAP or MES to the core systems.

Run old and new platforms in parallel, retiring an app only after its data and approval records are fully moved. The new platform will be chosen after the inventory, based on needs and budget.

AI in every department: rules first, then the right use cases

  • Pilot small: start inside IT, then scale once it works

Plan 4

Security is the baseline for integration and AI

The more systems are connected and the more widely AI is used, the higher the risk of data leaks and ransomware. I will get access rights, backups and network segmentation right first, as the foundation for integration and AI. I am an ISO 27001 Lead Auditor and have led a team through ISO 27001 certification.
